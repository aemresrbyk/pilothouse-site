Pilothouse

Privacy policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026 · Applies to the Pilothouse desktop app, the web app at usepilothouse.com/app, Pilothouse server and the Pilothouse iPhone app

In short: Pilothouse collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising and no crash reporting. We, the makers, never receive your code, prompts, terminal output, voice or any information about you.

What stays on your devices

Your projects, terminal output, agent conversations, settings, voice recordings and transcripts stay on your computer. Voice is transcribed on your computer (desktop) or on your iPhone (iPhone app, using Apple’s on-device speech recognition where your language supports it; otherwise Apple’s speech service handles it under Apple’s privacy policy).

The iPhone app stores only how to reach your computer and its own access keys, in the iPhone’s keychain. The web app stores the computers you paired and their keys in your browser’s local storage; “Forget” removes them.

What travels, and where

DataGoes toWhen
Agent tabs, the live terminal, approvals, messages, changes, to-dos, usage figuresBetween your computer and the phones and browsers you paired: either over your Tailscale network, or through the Pilothouse relay (a Cloudflare Worker). Through the relay every message is sealed with AES-256-GCM using keys that exist only on your computer and the paired device; the relay passes the sealed messages along and cannot read or change them. It stores nothing and keeps no logs. Cloudflare, which runs it, can see IP addresses and the size and timing of messages. While a device pairs, the relay passes its approximate location (city and country, as Cloudflare sees it) to your computer, which shows it next to the security code and does not keep it.Only if you turn on Remote control

A one-time download requestHugging FaceOnly if you turn on voice, to fetch the speech model

Your coding agents (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI and others) keep talking to their own providers exactly as they do without Pilothouse, under those providers’ policies. Pilothouse does not sit between them and does not read or use their login tokens.

Tailscale, Cloudflare, Apple, the browser push services, Hugging Face and GitHub are independent services with their own privacy policies.

On your computer

Pilothouse keeps a log of actions taken from your phone or browser in ~/.pilothouse/remote-audit.jsonl, on your computer only, so you can see what was approved and when. You can delete it at any time.

Children

Pilothouse is a developer tool and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. Because the apps are open source, every change is also visible on GitHub.
