What you won’t find elsewhere

Most tools pick one agent, or make you trust their server. Pilothouse is built for running several agents at once, from anywhere, without giving anything away.

Every agent, one Inbox

Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Antigravity and opencode side by side, across all your projects, computers and servers. Whoever needs you is on top.

Agents that work together

Out of plan usage? Another agent continues with the conversation, changed files and memory. Or let two agents debate a question in turns until they agree on a plan.

Approvals you can trust

Risky commands take two taps (and Face ID); a tap on a prompt that changed meanwhile is refused; every action is in an audit log on your computer.

From any browser, no account

Scan one QR code and use usepilothouse.com/app or the iPhone app: the live terminal, approvals, diffs. End-to-end encrypted; the relay can’t read a thing.

Brainstorm out loud

Talk an idea through with your agent by voice, on-device. When you agree, it writes the plan to SPEC.md, and a queue runs the tasks one by one or in parallel worktrees.

Yours, all the way

Free and MIT licensed. Your agents keep their own logins; Pilothouse never touches tokens or sits between you and your provider. View-only links, server mode, no telemetry.

Built for running agents side by side

Every project gets a real terminal tab that starts your agent with the flags, model and effort you choose. Stop losing track of which one is waiting.

Tabbed, real terminals

One login shell per tab, side by side in a grid if you like. Line tasks up in a queue: the next one goes in when the agent finishes. A worktree tab gives a second agent its own git branch.

Any agent, your own models

Claude Code, Codex, Antigravity, Gemini CLI or opencode per project. Pick the model and effort level from what your own account offers.

One Inbox

Everything your agents ask, with their own options as buttons (or press 1–9). Failed commands are listed apart, and you see each tab’s CPU and memory.

Hand-off between agents

Hit your plan’s limit? Continue with another agent. It starts with the last conversation, the changed files and the project memory.

History across agents

Claude Code and Codex conversations in one searchable list. Resume one, or hand that exact conversation to another agent.

Finds everything itself

On first launch it finds your agent CLIs and the projects they worked in. You pick the projects and the agent for each.

Your agents,
from anywhere.

Answer them from the iPhone app or any browser at usepilothouse.com/app. Scan one QR code in Pilothouse once and the phone stays paired: no account, no VPN to set up, end-to-end encrypted. (Already on Tailscale? That works too.)

  • Every agent, one list. What needs you is on top, across all your computers and servers.
  • The live terminal, exactly as on your desktop, with the keys an agent needs (Esc, arrows, Tab, Ctrl+C).
  • Approve safely. The agent’s own options as buttons. Risky commands (rm -rf, force-push, deploy, sudo) take two taps and Face ID; a tap on a prompt that changed meanwhile is refused.
  • Usage. How much of Claude Code’s 5-hour and weekly limits (and Codex’s) you have used, with reset times. The bar fills as you use it up.
  • Agent debate from your phone. Pick the agents and a question, follow their turns, stop it any time. Read-only, each agent on its own account.
  • Changes and to-dos. A tab’s uncommitted diff; a project’s TODOs and open checkboxes.
  • Talk instead of typing, recognised on the iPhone. Widgets on the home and lock screen.
  • Auto-approve rules for the commands you always allow. Never for risky ones.
  • View-only links to let someone watch, and a server mode for agents on a Linux box or VPS.

Talk to your agent.
Nothing leaves your computer.

Press ⌘⇧Space (Ctrl+Shift+Space on Windows and Linux) anywhere, or say “Hey Pilothouse”, and speak. Whisper large-v3-turbo transcribes it on your computer and types it into the active tab. Conversation mode reads the answer back, asks you to repeat words it wasn't sure about, and lets you approve with “yes” or “no”. Brainstorm mode (the light bulb) talks an idea through with your agent, then has it write the plan to SPEC.md.

Free. Open source. Collects nothing.

No subscription, no account, no analytics, no telemetry. Your code, prompts and terminal output stay on your computer and the devices you paired. Your agents talk to their own providers as they always do; Pilothouse never sits in between or touches their logins.

On your machine

Voice is transcribed locally. Usage limits are read from files the agent CLIs already keep.

Between your devices

End-to-end encrypted with keys only your computer and your paired devices hold. The relay in between only passes sealed messages and keeps nothing. Each device has its own key you can remove any time.

Install

macOS builds are signed and notarized by Apple. Every build updates itself.

brew install --cask aemresrbyk/tap/pilothouse
curl -fsSL https://raw.githubusercontent.com/aemresrbyk/pilothouse/main/install.sh | bash

Homebrew is for macOS; the script works on macOS and Linux (it installs the AppImage). On Windows, download Pilothouse-Setup-x64.exe; the build isn’t code-signed yet, so SmartScreen asks once (More info → Run anyway).
